## 4. Kurzrock "N-of-1 patient-centric clinical trials"

Summary (from PubMed-retrieved abstracts; DOIs below):
Advanced cancers are "extraordinarily complex and individually distinct" (about 95% of I-PREDICT patients had unique molecular landscapes), so matching one driver to one drug yields low matching and response rates. Kurzrock's model inverts the trial: instead of a *drug-centric* design testing one agent in a histology-defined group, a *patient-centric* N-of-1 design profiles each patient (tissue/blood NGS, immune biomarkers), a multidisciplinary **molecular tumor board** proposes a **customized combination** that co-targets as many pathogenic alterations as possible, and doses are individualized by starting low and using **intrapatient dose escalation/titration**, even for combinations never studied in phase I. The degree of matching is quantified as a **Matching Score** (alterations targeted / total pathogenic alterations); higher scores independently predict disease control, PFS and OS. Trials run under a **master protocol / master registry** so each patient's tailored regimen is still prospective, and real-world data plus tumor-agnostic baskets feed the same paradigm. Flagship studies: I-PREDICT (NCT02534675), WINTHER, and the UCSD MTB real-world cohort.

- Fountzilas, Tsimberidou, Vo, Kurzrock 2024, Cancer Treat Rev: "optimized treatment often requires drug combinations and N-of-1 customization, addressed by a new generation of N-of-1 trials." [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ctrv.2024.102703)
- Sicklick…Kurzrock 2019 I-PREDICT, Nat Med: "customized multidrug regimens…higher 'matching score', was correlated with significantly improved disease control rates". [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-019-0407-5)
- Sicklick…Kurzrock 2026 I-PREDICT, JCO: "individually dosed drug regimens customized to cotarget multiple molecular alterations"; "intrapatient dose-finding"; "safe and active N-of-1 matched treatment". [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO-25-01453)
- Kato…Kurzrock 2020, Nat Commun: MTB "N-of-One treatments…under the auspices of a master protocol"; "High (≥50%) versus low (<50%) Matching Score". [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-18613-3)
- Adashek, Subbiah, Kurzrock 2020, Trends Cancer: "From Tissue-Agnostic to N-of-One Therapies". [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trecan.2020.08.009)
- Nikanjam, Kato, Sicklick, Kurzrock 2023, Eur J Cancer: "personalised (N-of-1) dosing"; "intrapatient dose escalation". [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejca.2023.113359)
- Louie…Kurzrock 2024, iScience: "Molecular Tumor Board for Unicorns" (rare/ultra-rare cancers, N-of-One). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2024.110465)

## 5. CCSG reviewer criteria: retreats, collaboration, translation, trials

Current NOFO is PAR-25-444 (https://files.simpler.grants.gov/opportunities/45ab10e1-1758-4a2f-ad07-e93d95c5bedc/attachments/03c4caa9-3d31-4786-bbfd-cc42762e4ca6/PAR-25-444-Full-Announcement.html); the previous PAR-20-043 (https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/pa-files/par-20-043.html) carries identical language for the items below.

- **Transdisciplinary mandate**: Comprehensive centers must "have substantial transdisciplinary research that bridges these scientific areas" (basic; clinical; prevention/control/population). Purpose is "integrating meritorious research into a single transdisciplinary research enterprise across all institutional boundaries."
- **Retreats and working groups as evidence**: applicants must "Provide a brief description of how the Center fosters transdisciplinary collaboration through collaborative research projects, joint publications, retreats, working groups, colloquia, joint seminar series, and other types of meaningful interchange that cement interactions around related or common goals." Planning & Evaluation funds may cover "retreats designed to stimulate transdisciplinary research opportunities; and the regular assessment of Center goals and activities by the senior leadership."
- **Program review criteria** (PAR-25-444 §V, verbatim): "What is the quality of Program's cancer-relevant research evidenced by…: Papers in leading journals & widely cited work in the field? Paradigm-shifting hypotheses or methods advancing the field…? Progression through the translational pipeline? How collaborative is Program within the Center (e.g. inter-/intra-program effort), with other NDCCs and external partners…? What is the evidence that shows research relevant to the CA [catchment area] is addressed appropriately…? For Programs with clinical trials: What is the impact of trials and how well do trials…address cancer-related needs in the CA? How successful is the Program in translating basic science to clinical & population research, & vice versa? What evidence shows leadership in NCI-funded clinical trial networks and how appropriate is overall accruals to those trials?"
- **Required program data**: "The total number of Program publications and the percent that is intra- and inter-programmatic and/or collaborative with investigators in other institutions"; "A list of intra- and inter- programmatic activities and external collaborations…e.g., meetings, seminars, multi-investigator grants and publications, retreats, and working groups". Member roles must note "authorship of clinical trials, accrual of patients to interventional trials, and leadership roles in NCI NCTN studies."
- **Translational pipeline**: "Centers should ensure that they are both fostering basic discovery and, as applicable, facilitating transition of scientific findings through the translational pipeline (i.e., basic to pre-clinical and early clinical development, then to Phase III trials…)."
- **Investigator-initiated trials**: "A Cancer Center is a major source of innovative investigator-initiated clinical studies that can be exported to NCI's NCTN or other appropriate externally peer-reviewed funded mechanisms. Clinical studies involve relevant laboratory research whenever possible."
- **Strategic plan and EAC**: Leadership, Planning & Evaluation "should describe the general processes used by the Center to obtain effective internal and external advice, set priorities, make decisions, and define and evaluate Center strategic plans and activities. The Center should have a formal standing External Advisory Committee (EAC)… meet at least once yearly, and provide objective evaluation and advice in a consensus report to the Center Director". Shared Resources and CRTEC are also scored on "alignment to strategic plan".
- **Historical numeric benchmarks** (PAR-11-005, no longer in current text but still widely used by EACs): "Of total publications, 10% inter- and 10% intra- programmatic publications constitute general benchmarks for a reasonably interactive Program" and "A total of 10% accrual to therapeutic and prevention trials constitutes a general benchmark for a reasonable level of protocol accrual." — https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/pa-files/par-11-005.html
